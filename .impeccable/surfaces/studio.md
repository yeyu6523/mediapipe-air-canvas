# Studio surface

Scope: index.html, styles.css, src/*.js. Mode: Operate with an interactive drawing canvas.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A usable drawing studio where the large working canvas demonstrates hand-controlled marks. All controls surround the canvas instead of preceding it in a marketing page.

OWN-WORLD: Pale blue studio shell, white drawing paper, deep navy text, cobalt primary controls, warm vermilion ink. Fine borders, squared 12px panel corners, small precision SVG icons; Chinese UI uses the system CJK sans for legibility.

STORY: The visitor understands pinch-to-draw, starts a camera or a clearly identified alternative, creates marks, then exports their work. Actual hand tracking is visible in a small camera monitor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact top navigation, one line title and help, a broad 16:10 drawing sheet to the left, a 280px camera-and-brush rail on the right. A compact strip inside the sheet supports hand dwell color selection. The start camera action appears in the empty sheet and source panel.

FORM: Purpose-built working studio; code-led implementation selected to fulfill the user's delegated design task. No comp or seed lottery was run; the brief delegates design decisions. Signature interaction: the hand cursor visibly fills a dwell bar to change ink before a pinch produces a stroke.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
